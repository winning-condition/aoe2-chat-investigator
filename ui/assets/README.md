The parchment texture is reused from the user-supplied AOE2 Counter App,
`memory-game-app/assets/backgrounds/parchment-tile@2x.jpg`.

Cinzel (600 SemiBold) and Alegreya (400 Regular, 700 Bold) are bundled locally
from that app's `@expo-google-fonts` packages. Their SIL Open Font License
texts are included alongside them. No remote fonts, CDN or analytics are used.

The small document favicon and control icons are original code-native SVGs.
